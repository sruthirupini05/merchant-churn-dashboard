# Candidate Brief — CX AI-Proficiency Build Round

## The Task

Build a simple dashboard that flags merchants at risk of churning and recommends a next step for each one. You define everything: the merchant record shape, which signals you believe indicate churn risk, and what the recommended next step looks like. No historical data or dataset is provided; you decide what a merchant's record contains and why the metrics you pick actually matter.

Nothing further is specified beyond that.
